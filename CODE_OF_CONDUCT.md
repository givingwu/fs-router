# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

We welcome participants of every background and experience level. This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces, including people representing the project. Critique code and ideas respectfully; explain context, accept corrections, and make room for newcomers. Chinese and English are both welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, retaliation against reporters, and disruptive spam are unacceptable. Technical disagreement and reporting a bug are not misconduct.

## Report a concern

For project conduct concerns, contact repository owner **Giving Wu (`givingwu`)** at [givingwu@gmail.com](mailto:givingwu@gmail.com), using the subject `fs-router conduct report`. Include the relevant public links, what happened and how you prefer to be contacted; share only what is needed. Do not post private reports in an issue.

This address was verified as publicly listed on the [owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/givingwu) on 2026-09-12. It is an ordinary owner contact, not a staffed hotline; delivery and response time have not been tested or guaranteed. It is not designated as a private vulnerability inbox: software vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

If the concern involves the owner, the contact is unavailable, or the behavior violates GitHub's rules, use [GitHub's abuse reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam), including its direct support contact form. Reports to GitHub are handled by GitHub, not a separate project appeal panel. We do not claim to have an independent moderation team.

## Handling reports

The owner is responsible for project moderation. Review the available evidence, seek clarification where needed, and choose a proportionate response: request an edit or apology, give a warning, remove harmful content, restrict a thread, or restrict participation for repeated or serious conduct. Urgent harmful content may be removed before discussion. Explain the reason to the affected person where feasible, without exposing reporters or private details.

Share reports only as needed to investigate and act; do not promise absolute confidentiality. A participant may request reconsideration through the same contact with new evidence. When the owner is involved, GitHub's reporting route is available for platform-policy concerns. No response or resolution deadline is promised. Decisions must address behavior, never punish a good-faith technical report or disagreement.
